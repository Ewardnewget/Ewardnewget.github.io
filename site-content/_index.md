I am a Postdoctoral Researcher and Special Research Assistant at the Institute of Software, Chinese Academy of Sciences (ISCAS). I received my B.S. degree from Peking University in 2019 and my Ph.D. degree in Software Engineering from the Institute of Software, Chinese Academy of Sciences in 2025. Since July 2025, I have continued my research at ISCAS, working with Prof. Yan Cai as my postdoctoral advisor.

My research focuses on software analysis and testing, with a particular emphasis on analysis and testing of concurrent software. I am also interested in memory safety, fuzz testing, blockchain and AI4SE.
