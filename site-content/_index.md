I am a **postdoctoral researcher and Special Research Assistant** at the
**Institute of Software, Chinese Academy of Sciences**, where I have been working
with [Prof. Yan Cai](https://lcs.ios.ac.cn/~yancai/) since July 2025.

My research focuses on **software analysis and testing**, with an emphasis on
**concurrent software security**. My work includes sound data race prediction and
concurrency bug prediction. I am also interested in memory safety, fuzzing,
and blockchain.

I received my Ph.D. in Software Engineering from the Institute of Software,
Chinese Academy of Sciences in July 2025, supervised by Prof. Yan Cai.
Before that, I completed my undergraduate studies in Computer Science and
Technology at **Peking University** in June 2019.
